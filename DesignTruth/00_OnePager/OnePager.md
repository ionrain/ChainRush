# Slime Lords — One Pager

## Platform
Mobile (Android, iOS), focus on phones, portrait orientation.

## Genre
Auto-battler + Puzzle (swipe/tap grid).

## Setting / Narrative
Fantasy. Human heroes (mages, fighters) fight dark forces using slime-based summons.
Slimes allow modular visual variety that scales with selected tile chain size/level.

## Player Fantasy
“I’m a hero commander who shapes the battle in real time by quickly selecting the right slime/skill tiles.”

## Core Loop (session)
1) Battle runs automatically in the top half (waves from right to left).
2) Player interacts with the bottom grid using tap/swipe chains to obtain:
   - units, upgrades, hero abilities, boosters, gold
3) Grid refreshes on a timer → player must decide quickly.
4) Survive/complete level objective → rewards → meta progression.

## Core Inputs
- Tap single tile
- Swipe chain of matching tiles (selection breaks on mismatch/invalid tile)

## Win Conditions (level objective types)
- Survive for X time
- Survive X waves
- Reach a point while moving right
- Destroy target object (e.g., chest) to obtain artifact
- Destroy enemy structure on far right

## Lose Condition
- Protected object on the left side is destroyed (base/people/structure).

## Constraints / Non-Goals
- No micro-positioning of units in battle
- No complex manual control in the top half beyond grid-driven effects
- Puzzle is not a “match-3 with gravity”; it is a timed selection grid
